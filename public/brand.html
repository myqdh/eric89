■ Brand values

01 — 04

Clarity

Keep things simple. People should only see what they actually need - nothing extra.

Care

Take the small stuff seriously - spacing, wording, timing. It all adds up.

Kindness

Think about how people feel first, not just how a screen looks.

Moving Forward

Keep going - from a rough idea all the way to something people can actually use.

■ Look & feel

Keep it simple: a warm background, one bold color and a mark that still looks good whether it's tiny - a favicon - or huge - a poster. Everything follows the same rule as the design work itself - clean layouts, plenty of space, and one clear focus on every screen.
